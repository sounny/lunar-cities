# Lunar Cities

A hex-grid Moon city builder game. Pick one of five lunar city-states, then place
buildings on the board and score your neighborhood.

## The five cities

- **Bigan** - the feel-good leisure city
- **Kali** - the pirate city
- **Ogun** - the factory city
- **Khonsou** - the stopover city
- **Quetzal** - the city of science

Each city has its own color, philosophy, and gameplay bonus.

## How to play

1. Pick your city.
2. Choose a building: Habitat Dome, Greenhouse, Science Lab, Solar Array, Factory,
   Landing Pad, or Fun Park.
3. Place it on a free hex. Craters are blocked.
4. Score points for buildings, habitats side by side, solar power coverage, and
   building mix. Reach 60 points or fill the board to win.
5. Fill your Lunar Citizen Passport: rank the five cities in order, collect the
   four stamps (Build, Dream, Imagine, Discuss), and finish with "See you on
   the Moon?"

Controls: Undo, Eraser, Clear, and Surprise me. Play again any time.

## About

A single self-contained HTML file, no build step, no tracking. Inspired by the
"Lunar Cities" build-your-own-Moon-neighborhood museum exhibit.

Play it here: https://sounny.github.io/lunar-cities/
