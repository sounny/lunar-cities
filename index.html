4How to play

  1. Pick a city. Each city has its own bonus. Choose the one you like best.
  2. Pick a building from the Buildings list.
  3. Tap a hex on the Moon board to place it. Gray hatched hexes are craters. Nothing can go there.
  4. Score points. Habitats like neighbors. Buildings next to solar arrays get power points. A good mix scores more.
  5. Win by reaching 60 points, or by filling every spot on the board.
  6. Fill your passport. Rank the five cities and collect the four stamps: Build, Dream, Imagine, Discuss.
Made a mistake? Use the Eraser, or tap Undo last. Want a new start? Tap Play again or Clear board.
